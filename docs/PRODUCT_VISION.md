# Product Vision

## Mission

Core exists to make rehabilitation accessible, consistent, and even enjoyable. By providing a beautiful daily companion that answers the question "What do I need to do today?" instantly, Core removes friction from the rehabilitation process and helps people build lasting healthy habits.

## Problem Being Solved

Abdominal rehabilitation programs are typically delivered on paper handouts or through clinical-grade software that feels sterile and uninviting. Users struggle with:

- Remembering which day they're on
- Knowing what exercises to do
- Tracking their consistency
- Staying motivated over 18+ weeks
- Understanding their progress

## Target Users

- Individuals recovering from abdominal surgery
- Postpartum individuals rebuilding core strength
- Anyone following a structured core rehabilitation program
- Healthcare providers who want to give patients a digital companion

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity First** — The app should answer "What do I need to do today?" immediately
2. **Calm & Beautiful** — The experience should feel like Apple Health met Calm
3. **Encouraging, Never Clinical** — Warm, supportive language throughout
4. **Data-Driven** — Programs loaded from data files, never hardcoded
5. **Progressive Enhancement** — Start simple, add features without redesign

## Long-Term Vision

- **v1.x:** Full client-side app with tracking, measurements, achievements
- **v2.x:** Cloud sync, authentication, clinician portal
- **v3.x:** AI-powered adaptive coaching, multi-program support
- **Future:** Research partnerships, outcome tracking, community features

## Success Criteria

- Users open the app daily without friction
- Program completion rates exceed 60%
- Users report feeling supported and motivated
- The codebase supports rapid feature addition
